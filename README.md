# Unsub

Unsub is a web-based game with the goal of unsubscribing from a mysterious newsletter!

# Try it out!

[Here!](https://unsub-vert.vercel.app)

# To start:

Simply enter your name (whatever you'd like to be called!) and click "Unsubscribe"! This starts the introduction.
After reading the intro, please click on the "link" provided, and you'll start the game!

## Features:
The game features a few different levels! 
Here they are:

Confirmation page; after clicking unsubscribe, you're taken to this page to confirm! One sneaky thing - it's impossible to confirm! The buttons switch places to conveniently make you click "No"!

Clicky page; after you are tricked into clicking "No", you have to click this button 20 times to verify!

Shmessay page - my personal favourite! After you've painstakingly spammed the button, you have to write a persuasive essay, marked by a sassy AI model (GPT-OSS-20B). If you're interested in seeing the system prompt, please visit the /api page. (it'd probably benefit from a revised version!)

Terms of Shrimp! (my personal LEAST FAVOURITE! XD)
Firstly, the name: I wanted to call it Terms of Service (like any other website) but figured - to spare me from any potential legal trouble, to call it something else. Not sure why, but my brain came up with "shrimp" first (10pm coding sessions do weird things to you) and it stuck!

After receiving your (slightly hurtful) grade, you click to continue, which takes you to your TOS!
The objective is to click on each (10px, MOVING) button. There are 58 in total, all of which contain some random things that I thought up, which - yes, took long. XD
The reason it's my least favourite is because I had to test it so many times (including to get the next screenshot :P) to make sure browsers don't break it. (PS, "tab" is disabled here, so don't even try it! Thanks to my friend for reminding me of that!)

# Other information:

I thought of this idea as a sequel to my previous "useless" genre of projects. 
The goal is to unsubscribe from a suspicious company's persistent newsletter! As always, they make it as complicated as possible! The game features several "stages" of verification. I won't spoil any more! Go play it! :D

Important note; the company in the game - "Fraude" is fictional and does not represent nor is affiliated with any real companies. The name is simply the French word for "fraud".
